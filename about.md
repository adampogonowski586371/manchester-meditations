# About

Manchester Meditations is a small, ongoing collection of Stoic reflections — written by someone living an ordinary life in Manchester, trying to apply old philosophy to the weather, the commute, the people, and the quiet daily tests of temperament.

Nothing here is academic. It's just an attempt to notice what is and isn't up to me, one Manchester day at a time.

Posts are written and published here directly, no fuss, no algorithm.
