Before the shutters go up, the Northern Quarter belongs to the people who actually live there. A street sweeper. A woman walking a dog too big for her. The smell of bread from somewhere unseen.

By ten it'll be tourists and brunch queues. But at seven it's just brick, quiet, and a kind of stillness the city rarely shows anyone.
