It rains here in a way that feels less like weather and more like a mood the city is in. Oxford Road this morning was all umbrellas and bus exhaust, students cutting between buildings, the wet stone going a shade darker than usual.

There's a particular kind of comfort in a city that doesn't pretend otherwise. Nobody's surprised. Nobody complains. The rain is just Tuesday.
