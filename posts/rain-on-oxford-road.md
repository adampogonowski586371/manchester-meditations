title: Rain on Oxford Road
date: 2026-07-20
excerpt: The rain doesn't ask my permission before it falls, and there's a strange relief in finally accepting that.
tags: post

Oxford Road this morning: umbrellas up, bus exhaust, students cutting between buildings, the wet stone gone a shade darker than usual. Nothing about it was asking to be enjoyed. It rained because it rains, indifferent to whether I had an umbrella or the right shoes.

Epictetus has a line I keep coming back to on mornings like this: some things are up to us, and some are not. The rain is not up to me. My scowling at it, my hurrying with my collar up cursing the sky, that part is entirely mine to choose, and mostly unnecessary.

So I let it fall. I noticed the smell of wet pavement, the specific grey of a Manchester sky that never quite commits to storm or clear. There is a kind of freedom in refusing to argue with the weather.
