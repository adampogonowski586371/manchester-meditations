Belonging isn't handed to you by a birth certificate. It's built, brick by brick, the same way the city was.

You start knowing which bus is late on purpose. Then which bakery does the good stuff on Fridays. Then, eventually, you stop describing yourself as "living in Manchester" and start saying "I'm from here," even if you weren't, and even if a small part of you still feels like you're getting away with something.

That's not dishonesty. That's just what belonging actually is.
